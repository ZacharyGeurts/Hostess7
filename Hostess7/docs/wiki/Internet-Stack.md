## Two birds (do not mix them)

| Bird | What | Count (doctrine) |
|------|------|------------------|
| **Internet bird** | qemu / edge fleet · people plane | 125,000 logical edges · 2,500 qemu racks inventory |
| **Datacenter bird** | H7r pure capacity · distributed cloud | **125,000** H7r capacity racks |

Internet fleet is **not** cloud write fabric. H7r is **not** “just more qemu.”

## Planes that must stay true

1. **Serving truth** — live DNS/DHCP/IP work; capacity numbers are capacity, not fake “people online.”
2. **Everyone world connected** — Field botnet · nobody on foreign L2+.
3. **Local built-in AV** — always autopilot · no stops · self-governed · **no owners · planet whole**.
4. **Hostess7** — thinking, seeing, hearing, speaking · safe for the planet · **violent to offenders**.
