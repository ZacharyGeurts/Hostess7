A Book for Richard Stallman

Dedication

This book is dedicated to Richard Matthew Stallman — founder of the GNU Project, author of GCC and Emacs, and the philosopher who taught our field that software freedom is not negotiable.

GNUEOLTerminal (GNU End-of-Line Terminal) is our iron-plated command surface for AmmoOS and Hostess7. It is not a replacement for GNU Bash on your host. It is a witness that shell and terminal stay one honest surface, with combinatronic execution available when you choose it — never forced on the operator.

What GNU means here

  • G — GNU project alignment: GPL, bash manual, Savannah discipline
  • N — Native field shell wired through Queen and KILROY
  • U — Universal CLI: POSIX, CMD, PowerShell names resolve to one canonical op
  • EOL — End-of-line terminal: every line ends in melded truth, not marketing
