## Chapter 1 — Model vs metal

![Planet](h7fig:planet)

Typical board numbers:

- **359** regions / areas  
- **6000** racks per region (model)  
- **~2.154e6** total racks defending  

These are **field model** scales for Big Grin / UP / world-planet fleets. They are not
a claim that a laptop contains two million metal racks. Every briefing must say
**model vs metal**.
