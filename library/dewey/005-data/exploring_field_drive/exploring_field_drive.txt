# Exploring Field Drive

![Cover](h7fig:cover)

**Plain H7 · Dewey 005**  
**Companion:** Exploring FFAT Storage · Exploring Shannon on the Field  

A Field Drive is media with **identity**, not only free space. This book trains Hostess 7
and operators to probe, ensure, and refuse false volumes while keeping study copies on
TEAM drive and GitHub.

## Chapter 1 — Identity tags

If a host mounts the volume as MS-DOS vfat and smiles, that is not field identity.
Field tools speak FFAT/FDRV. Hostess 7 should refuse to call foreign vfat “Field Drive.”

## Chapter 2 — Probe ritual

![Probe](h7fig:probe)

1. `ffat_probe(path)` — read without write.  
2. Classify: clean, FFAT v4, legacy, foreign, fake-factor legacy.  
3. Only then `ffat_ensure` with eyes open.  

Legacy fake ×91 superblocks must be rewritten away. Prefer `guaranteed_bytes` over
stories. Never run destructive ensure on unknown disks.

## Chapter 3 — Frames and map

Frames default to 4096 bytes. Map kinds FREE/ZERO/RAW/RLE/REF/PAK/DICT decide pool
spend. `ffat_put_frame` / `ffat_get_frame` are C++ IO. Narrate one write path without
inventing ratios. After writes, read `pack_ratio` and mean Shannon.
